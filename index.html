Four small experiments

Training curves, samples and numbers from one afternoon on one GPU.

MNIST

A convolutional neural network with 80,602 parameters trained on MNIST for 1,400 iterations in 9.3 seconds. The model reached 98.75% accuracy on the test set.

GAN

A DCGAN with 1.73 million parameters trained on CIFAR-10 for 177 epochs in 300 seconds. The generator reached a diversity ratio of 1.07 by the final epoch, with the strip below showing one row of fixed-noise samples per epoch.

Hash-grid image

Instant-NGP with 8.4 million parameters fit to a 2D image over 9,392 training steps in 240 seconds. The model achieved a PSNR of 39.9 dB on wave.jpg.

Shakespeare

A character-level transformer with 818,048 parameters trained on tiny Shakespeare for 59,358 steps in 240 seconds. The model achieved a validation loss of 1.48.

Each experiment lives in its own repo: mnist-cnn, dcgan-cifar10, instant-ngp-2d and tiny-shakespeare. Set in Georgia and the system sans.
